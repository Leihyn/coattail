Coattail

Track records nobody can fake. Then ride the good ones.

Copy trading fails on one problem: the record is self-reported. Anyone can claim a hit rate, delete the losses, or show you a screenshot. Coattail removes the claim entirely. A trader's position is readable straight off their signature, so the record is something you compute, not something you are told.

Built on Discreet Log Contracts. Everything here runs in this tab: BIP-340 oracle attestation, anticipation points, and adaptor signatures whose bound outcome an observer can recover. No server, no node, no wallet.

1Open a market

An oracle announces a question it will later answer, and nothing else. Two outcomes.

2Three traders take a position

Each picks a side privately and publishes only an adaptor signature. They tell you nothing. The table below is what an observer can work out anyway, by testing the signature against each outcome's anticipation point. Exactly one will verify.

3The market resolves

The oracle publishes one number. Every position settles against it, and the record updates itself. Nobody reports anything.

4The leaderboard, and riding it

Run several markets and a record accumulates that no one authored. Then copy the leader: take the same side they took on the open market, by building your own signature against the same point.

Resolve a market first.

What you still have to take on trust

The oracle, to answer the question honestly. Nothing else. Not the traders, who cannot misreport a position they never reported. Not a platform, which holds nothing and could not edit the record if it wanted to. The record is a function of signatures anyone can recompute.

Implemented: BIP-340 oracle attestation, anticipation point derivation, adaptor signature creation, recovery of the bound outcome by an observer, settlement, and record accumulation. Not implemented: funding transactions, CET construction, broadcast, and multi-outcome markets. This page proves the mechanism; it does not move coins.
